# Code of Conduct

> English counterpart of [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). | [Türkçe](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

This project aims to maintain a respectful, safe, and technically productive community.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect different levels of experience, identities, and viewpoints.
- Direct criticism toward code, evidence, and behavior—not people.
- Treat security and financial risks seriously.
- Correct misinformation clearly when you identify it.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused, reproducible, and free of secrets.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, or degrading language
- Sharing personal information, credentials, or private communications without permission
- Seeking support for market manipulation, fraud, or unlawful use
- Publishing a vulnerability while bypassing the responsible-disclosure process
- Spam, deliberate misinformation, or sabotage of community work

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through GitHub. Use [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md) for security incidents. The maintainer may remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently exclude a participant.

These rules apply to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces operated in the project's name. Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith based on context, impact, and recurrence.
